Our story

A dream between partners

Battle Born Custom Trailers was founded by David and Oliver Mendoza to build custom food trailers — and to help people become business owners.

One year in

Building futures, not just trailers

One year ago this all started with a dream between business partners — a vision to build custom food trailers and help others start their own businesses.

What began as an idea quickly turned into a mission: helping people bring their food concepts to life and guiding them through the process of becoming business owners.

Through hard work, dedication, and faith, we've been blessed with an incredible first year. We're truly grateful for every client who trusted us, supported us, recommended us, and let us be part of their journey.

This is only the beginning. Year 2 — we're building even bigger.
